Privacy Policy

We collect nothing. Ablate Chat has no accounts on our servers, no analytics and no telemetry.

  • Your profile, chats, memories and settings are stored in a local SQLite database, ~/.ablate/chat/ablate.db, on your computer. Delete that folder to erase them.
  • Prompts and answers are processed by the model on your own CPU or GPU. They are never sent to AI Anytime or anyone else.
  • Network use: the app goes online only to download a model from the HuggingFace Hub when you click Download, and to fetch a web page if you turn on network tools and the model calls them. HuggingFace's own privacy policy applies to downloads.
  • Sharing on your network: if you start the app with --share, other devices on your network can sign in with your passcode. Their chats are stored on your computer.
